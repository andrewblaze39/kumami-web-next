## Bugs found and fixed

1. **Duplicate React keys on the Calendar.** The macro feed sometimes lists the same release twice (same name, country and time), and both copies got the same id. **Fix:** `mergeCalendarEvents` de-duplicates by id (`src/lib/market/rules/calendarEvents.ts`), plus a unit test. This was introduced by the 9 Oct Calendar build.
2. **"Spot buying $0M in 4H" on Spot Pulse alert cards** (On-Chain Insights). Amounts under $500K were rounded to whole millions. **Fix:** amounts are now formatted by size ($B / $M / $K / $) in `src/lib/market/live/spotPulse.ts`.

## Observations (exploratory pass)

- Day cells hold 20–80 macro prints, so most cells end in "+N more", with no way to open a day and see the rest. The impact filter is the only way through. Worth a day-detail view (Rachelle's spec also asks for day/week views).
- The first page load after a dev-server start can take more than 45 seconds while the market cache fills. Once warm, `/api/market/console` answers in about 2 seconds. That's a dev-only effect, but a cold production cache would also make the first user wait.
