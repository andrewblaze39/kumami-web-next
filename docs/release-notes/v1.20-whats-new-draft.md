## A · Live in this update

**Console**
- A heads-up bar now appears at the top of your Console when a high-impact event the Kumami team flagged starts within 6 hours, for example "CPI in 4h — expect volatility". Tap it to open the Calendar.

**On-Chain Insights**
- HYPE is now on the coin switcher (it replaces AVAX), so all five core coins have their own on-chain view.
- Funding Rate reads are more accurate. Normal funding no longer shows as "overheated", and the bands have no gaps.
- Exchange Netflow opens on the 7-day view, because exchange balances only update once a day.
- Open Interest has two new reads: "Trend Without Leverage" (price rising without new leverage) and "Decline Without Panic" (price falling without forced selling).

**Watchlist**
- "Short Heavy" now only appears when shorts are paying meaningfully (below −0.05% funding). It uses the same scale as the rest of Kumami.
- The regime tag uses real open-interest data, so "Coiling" (leverage building while price is flat) can now show.

**Kumami Pro**
- Daily Digest now also shows the biggest smart-money positions opened in the last 24 hours, today's "Also Worth Watching" coins, and today's Fear & Greed read.
- Price alerts now support HYPE, plus POL and RENDER (the new names of MATIC and RNDR). Alerts you set on the old names keep working.

## B · Coming next (publish only what's merged)

**Flow Radar**
- Clearer signals. Liquidations now say whether longs were flushed or shorts were squeezed. Smart-money rows say whether a position was opened long, opened short or closed. Whale transfers count only real deposits to and withdrawals from exchanges.
- New "Dry Powder" events: large stablecoin moves onto exchanges (buying power arriving) or off them.
- The market verdict needs one side to clearly outweigh the other, and adjusts to the time window you pick.
- Flow Radar Plus now shows the coloured "confirming" outlines too.
- Pro: very thin coins (under $1M of daily spot volume) are hidden to cut noise.

**Spot Pulse**
- More accurate reads. Each coin is compared with its own normal swing, the price thresholds scale with the time window, and a new "Quiet Distribution" state shows quiet spot selling while price is flat.
- New listings say "not enough history" instead of guessing.
- Pro: the second row now shows your "Also Worth Watching" coins, filled with the most-traded coins when fewer qualify.

**Watchlist Pro**
- Pin any liquid coin with a futures market, up to 15 (stablecoins and wrapped tokens excluded).
- "Also Worth Watching" now looks for coins with 3 days of real net buying, confirmed by spot buyers. It shows fewer coins rather than padding the list.
- Live prices on every row.

**Calendar**
- New event types: exchange listings and token sales / TGEs (with venue, raise and an official link).
- Airdrops with a claim date appear on the Calendar automatically.
- Shows high- and medium-impact events by default. Tap LOW to see everything.
