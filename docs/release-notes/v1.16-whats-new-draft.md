## Kumami update — October 2026

**Console**
- A heads-up bar appears at the top when a high-impact event the Kumami team flagged starts within 6 hours, for example "CPI in 4h — expect volatility". Tap it to open the Calendar.

**On-Chain Insights**
- HYPE replaces AVAX on the coin switcher.
- Funding Rate is more accurate. Normal funding no longer shows as "overheated", and the bands have no gaps.
- Exchange Netflow opens on the 7-day view.
- Open Interest has two new reads: "Trend Without Leverage" and "Decline Without Panic".
- **Spot Pulse** is smarter:
  - each coin is compared with its own normal swing, and the price thresholds scale with the time window;
  - a new "Quiet Distribution" read shows quiet spot selling while price is flat;
  - new listings say "not enough history" instead of guessing;
  - each tile shows 24h spot volume.

**Flow Radar**
- Clearer signals. Liquidations say whether longs were flushed or shorts squeezed. Smart-money rows say whether a position was opened long, opened short or closed. Whale transfers count only real deposits to and withdrawals from exchanges.
- New "Dry Powder" events: large stablecoin moves onto exchanges (buying power arriving) or off them.
- The verdict needs one side to clearly outweigh the other, and adjusts to the time window you pick.
- Flow Radar Plus now shows the coloured "confirming" outlines too.

**Fear & Greed**
- The score now leaves out any signal that has no data instead of guessing, and Price Momentum and Volatility use real daily prices.

**Calendar**
- New event types: exchange listings and token sales / TGEs, with venue, raise and an official link.
- High- and medium-impact events are shown by default. Tap LOW to see everything.
- Pro members also see airdrop claim dates.

**Watchlist**
- "Short Heavy" only appears when shorts are paying meaningfully.
- "Coiling" (leverage building while price is flat) can now show.

**Coming soon**
- AI-written reads under the Console regime and the Flow Radar verdict, and AI-scored news tone on Fear & Greed.

**Everywhere**
- The Basic / Plus / Pro switch shows plain words, and on phones the bottom bar is gone — use the menu button at the top.
- Spot Pulse: hover a coin to see whether price, spot buying and futures buying are up, flat or down.

**Kumami Pro**
- **Watchlist Pro:**
  - pin any liquid coin with a futures market, up to 15;
  - "Also Worth Watching" looks for coins with 3 days of real net buying, confirmed by spot buyers, and shows fewer coins rather than padding the list;
  - live prices on every row.
- **Spot Pulse Pro:** the second row shows your "Also Worth Watching" coins, filled with the most-traded coins when fewer qualify.
- **Flow Radar Pro:** very thin coins (under $1M of daily spot volume) are hidden to cut noise.
- **Daily Digest:** now shows the biggest smart-money positions opened in the last 24 hours, today's "Also Worth Watching" coins and today's Fear & Greed read.
- **Price alerts:** HYPE added, plus POL and RENDER (the new names of MATIC and RNDR). Alerts you set on the old names keep working.
